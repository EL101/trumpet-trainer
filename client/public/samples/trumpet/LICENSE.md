# Trumpet samples

The practice player plays exercises with these notes (concert pitch, a minor third
apart), repitched to the notes between. They're the trumpet from the Fluid (R3) General
MIDI SoundFont, as rendered to MP3 by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
(`FluidR3_GM/trumpet-mp3/`).
